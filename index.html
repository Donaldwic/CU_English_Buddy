Our Approach: Friendly Support, Real Answers

CU English Buddy makes asking English questions normal, not scary. We provide:

Guidance from University Students

Connect directly via private chat with experienced Chulalongkorn University students ('Pee Nisit Chula') who explain things clearly and simply.

AI-Verified Accuracy

Our AI helps ensure the answers you receive are accurate and reliable, giving you confidence in what you learn.

Stress-Free Private Chat

Ask anything about grammar or reading comprehension in a relaxed, one-on-one chat. We're like a helpful 'Pee' (older sibling), not a strict teacher.

Getting Help is Easy

Just follow these simple steps:

1. Type Your Question

Enter the platform and ask your question directly, like chatting with a friend. No complex forms.

2. Get Clear Answers

Receive easy-to-understand explanations, examples, or even short video clips that make sense instantly.

3. Learn & Connect

Explore optional mini-courses tailored to your needs or share tips and find study buddies in the community.
